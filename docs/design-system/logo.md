# Logo

The logo is the **palaver tree, drawn as a V**. In villages across West, Central and East
Africa people gather under a big tree to talk things through: the arbre à palabres in
Senegal and Côte d'Ivoire, the baraza tree in Kenya. That meeting place is the village
square, so the mark says what the product is, in English and in French. The two limbs
rise from one foot into a flat canopy, and the space between them is the V.

## Files

All files are in `logo/`, drawn on a 32 by 32 grid.

| File | Use it on | Colour |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `palaver-tree-v_laterite-700.svg` | Paper surfaces: headers, documents, light screens | Laterite-700 `#B23F0C` |
| `palaver-tree-v_laterite.svg` | Ink surfaces: dark screens, dark slides. Also the browser tab icon | Laterite `#F0601F` |
| `palaver-tree-v_on-paper.svg` | App icon, avatars, social images in light | Laterite-700 on a Paper square |
| `palaver-tree-v_on-ink.svg` | App icon, social images and anywhere a dark square suits | Laterite on an Ink square |

In code, colour the mark with `var(--logo)`. It is Laterite-700 on Paper and Laterite on
Ink, and switches with the theme.

These are the only two treatments. Laterite-700 on Paper is 5.82:1 and Laterite on Ink is
5.96:1, both well above the 3:1 a shape needs.

**One exception, the browser tab.** The tab icon is the Laterite mark on a clear background
(`palaver-tree-v_laterite.svg`), as on the approved landing page. It shows Laterite on
whatever colour the browser gives its tabs, including a light one (3.28:1 on white, still
above the 3:1 a shape needs). Tanny chose this on 10 November 2026. Everywhere else the rule
below stands.

## Clear space

Keep a quarter of the logo's size free on every side: 8 units of its 32-unit grid. At
32px that is 8px; at 64px it is 16px. Nothing goes in that space: no text, icons, edges
of the screen or other logos. The square files already include their own padding, so use
them as they are.

## Minimum size

16px on screen. The mark is drawn so its canopy and foot land on whole pixels at 16px,
which keeps it sharp in a browser tab. Below 16px, do not use it.

| Where | Size |
| --- | --- |
| Browser tab (favicon) | 16 and 32px, `laterite` file (see below) |
| App header on phones | 24px |
| App header on desktop | 32px |
| App icon, social image | 64px and up, a square file |

## The name next to the logo

A lockup of the mark and the name has not been drawn yet. Until it is, use the mark on
its own, and write "Village Sqwhere" as normal text nearby in Unbounded, the display
font. Do not build a lockup yourself.

## Do not

- Change its colours, or use any colour other than the two treatments. No Lagoon: that is
  the AI's colour.
- Put Laterite-700 on Ink (3.36:1) or Laterite on Paper as the logo.
- Add a square, a dot, a sparkle, a star, an outline, a shadow, a gradient or a glow.
- Stretch, squash, rotate, flip or crop it.
- Redraw it, or set it in a font.
- Place it on a photo or a busy background without a solid Paper or Ink area behind it.
- Animate it, except fading in or out.
- Use it smaller than 16px or inside its own clear space.
